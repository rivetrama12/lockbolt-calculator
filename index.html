Scrap Value Ratio — Nut Manufacturing

AISI 1010 / IS 1363  |  Wire Rod → Finished Nut  |  M8 worked example

Governing Equations

── Volumes ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Hex body vol: V_hex = (√3/2)·s²·H [s = across flats, H = nut height] Slug vol: V_slug = (π/4)·d_tap²·H [d_tap = tap drill dia] Net nut vol: V_nut = V_hex − (π/4)·d3²·H [d3 = minor thread dia] ── Mass Balance ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Wire rod in: W_in = (W_nut + W_slug) / (1 − f_proc) [f_proc = 3% losses] Total scrap: W_scr = W_in − W_nut ── Scrap Ratios ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Scrap wt ratio: SVR_w = W_scr / W_in × 100 [%] Scrap val ratio:SVR_v = (W_scr · P_scrap) / (W_in · P_rod) × 100 [%] Material eff.: η_mat = W_nut / W_in × 100 [%] Net mat. cost: C_net = W_in·P_rod − W_scr·P_scrap [₹/nut]
